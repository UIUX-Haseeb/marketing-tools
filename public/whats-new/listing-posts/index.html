What's New
New feature9 November 2026

Make listing, review and sales achievement posts in minutes

New listing, a property on the market, a closed deal or a fresh lease? Share it with a polished post in a few minutes. Add the property photo, fill in a few lines and download. Your name and photo are already on it, and so is the DLD permit QR.

Where to find itMarketing → Tools → Just ListedFor SaleJust SoldJust RentedGoogle ReviewSales Achievement

All four posts are made in the same tool, so once you've made one, you can make them all. No design files, no waiting: pick your listing, choose a look, and the post updates as you go. When it looks right, generate it and share it.

1. Open the post you need

Go to Marketing → Tools and click the one that fits:

PostUse it forWhat's on it
Just ListedA property you've just listedBed, bath and sq ft tags, an optional tagline, type, location and price
For SaleA property on the marketThe same layout as Just Listed, headed "For Sale"
Just SoldA deal you've closedBedrooms, type, location and price
Just RentedA property you've leasedBedrooms, type, location and the rent, quoted per year, month, week or day

2. Pick your listing

  1. Under Listing, search by the reference or the title and pick it. The property details, price and photo fill in for you, and so does the agent on that listing: their name, job title and headshot go on the post. You can still change all of it.
  2. Posting something that isn't in your listings? Skip this and type the details in yourself.

3. Choose the design and the format

  1. Under Design, pick Classic (everything centred on a frosted card) or Minimal (no card, so more of the photo shows).
  2. Under Format, pick Post for your Instagram and LinkedIn feed (1080 × 1440) or Story for Stories and WhatsApp Status (1080 × 1920). Switch as often as you like; your details stay put.

4. Check the property photo

  1. A listing with photos shows them in a strip under Listing ("33 photos on this listing — pick the one for the post"). The first one is used; click another to swap it. No photos on the listing, or want your own? Click Choose a photo or drag one in (JPG or PNG, up to 10 MB). Landscape or portrait both work; it's cropped to fit.
  2. Drag the photo in the preview to frame it, and use Size to zoom in. Reset puts it back.

5. Fill in anything that's missing

  1. Check the bedrooms, property type, location and price. On Just Listed and For Sale there are bathrooms and sq ft too, plus an optional Tagline printed under the headline (for example "Luxury 3BR | Full Marina Views | Dual Terraces").
  2. On Just Rented, pick how the rent is quoted (Per year, Per month, Per week or Per day) and it's printed after the price for you.

6. Check the agent

  1. If you picked a listing, the agent is the one on that listing, with their name, job title and headshot. Without a listing, it's you. Posting for someone else? Search for them under Agent.
  2. Replace the headshot if you like; it's cropped to the circle on the card. Drag inside the circle in the preview to move it.

7. Generate and share

  1. Click Generate … Post (for example Generate Just Listed Post). It downloads straight away, ready to post.

Google Review

Turn a client's Google review into a shareable post, with the rating, their words and the agent it's about.

  1. Go to Marketing → Tools and click Google Review. Pick Post or Story.
  2. Set the Rating (five stars to start), paste the Review exactly as the client wrote it, and add the Reviewer's name. Leave a blank line between paragraphs to keep them apart; long reviews shrink to fit.
  3. Under Agent, search for the colleague the review is about. Their job title comes from their portal record, and their portal photo is used for the headshot (you can replace it).
  4. Click Generate Review Post.

Sales Achievement

Celebrate a colleague's sales milestone with their photo over a project or skyline.

  1. Go to Marketing → Tools and click Sales Achievement. Under Stats, pick Total transactions or Primary & Secondary.
  2. Pick the Agent. Their portrait loads and its background is taken off right in your browser (the photo never leaves your computer; the first time on a device takes a little longer). Choose Removed or Original, and drag the photo in the preview to place the head and shoulders.
  3. Add a Background photo: a project or skyline shot that fills the whole post.
  4. Fill in the Achievement: Rank, Period, Total sale value and Transactions. It reads like "Secured #1 for Q3, closing on a high note with AED 80.3 Million in sales". Rank and period can be left out.
  5. Click Generate Sales Achievement Post.
Good to know
  • The DLD permit QR is already on the listing posts, so there's nothing to upload.
  • The little counters next to each field show how much room is left. Keep it short and it'll always fit nicely.
  • If the button is greyed out, the hint above it tells you what's missing, usually the property photo.
