What's New
New feature9 November 2026

Send a polished listing brochure in a few clicks

Sending a client a shortlist? Turn one listing, or up to 30, into a PDF brochure: a page for each property, then a closing page with your card or the listing agent's. Pick the pictures, choose navy or white, and download.

Where to find itMarketing → Tools → Listing Brochure

No more copying listing details into a design file. Everything on the brochure comes from the listing itself: the photos, the title, the price and the specs. You choose the pictures and whose card goes at the end, and the PDF is ready to send.

1. Add your listings

  1. Go to Marketing → Tools and click Listing Brochure.
  2. Under Add a listing, search by the reference or the title and pick it. Add as many as you like, up to 30. Each one gets its own page, in the order you add them, and the closing page comes last.

2. Pick the pictures

  1. Every property page has five photo spots: Top left, Top right and Bottom 1 to 3. Click a spot, then click the photo you want from the listing's pictures.
  2. Changed your mind? Reset pictures puts the first five back.

3. Choose whose card goes on the closing page

  1. Under Whose card, pick Me, Listing's agent or Colleague. The name, job title, phone and e-mail come from the portal record. The name can be shortened to fit; the job title can't be changed.
  2. Use Headshot size if the photo needs a nudge.

4. Navy or white

  1. Under Design, pick A · Navy or B · White. Every page switches together.

5. Download the PDF

  1. The bar above the preview shows how many pages you've got (for example "2 pages · 4:5 · 1080 × 1350"). Click Download PDF and send it on.
Good to know
  • Pages are 4:5 (1080 × 1350), so they look great on a phone, in WhatsApp and as images.
  • The closing page also tells clients what else Provident can help with: second citizenship, conveyancing and currency exchange.
  • Price, specs or photos wrong on a page? Those come from the listing, so fix them in Listings and add it again.
