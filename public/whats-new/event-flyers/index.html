New feature6 November 2026

Turn any event into a flyer with your contact card on it

Got an event coming up? Make a ready-to-share flyer in a few clicks. Pick the event, choose a story or a feed post, pick a look, and download. The event details are already filled in, and your photo, name and number sit at the bottom so the leads come straight to you.

Where to find itMarketing → Tools → Event Flyers

Sharing a launch, an open house or a webinar shouldn't mean waiting on a design or retyping the date and venue yourself. With Event Flyers you don't have to. Everything on the flyer comes straight from the event the marketing team set up, so it's always right, and your contact card makes it yours.

1. Pick your event

  1. Go to Marketing → Tools and click Event Flyers.
  2. You'll see the published events from Events, with the next one at the top. Type an event, venue or city into the search to narrow it down.
  3. Click the event. Its title, dates, venue and photo are filled in for you. Looking for one that's already happened? Turn on Include past events.

2. Choose the format and the look

  1. Under Format, pick Story 9:16 for Stories, Status and WhatsApp, or Post 4:5 for your Instagram and LinkedIn feed.
  2. Under Design, try A · Navy panel, B · Full photo or C · Paper. The preview updates straight away, so feel free to play around.

3. Check your contact card

  1. Add my contact card is on from the start. Your photo, name, BRN, job title, phone and e-mail go at the foot of the flyer, in place of the event contact, so the leads come to you.
  2. Your details come from your portal record, and your profile photo is used while Use my profile photo is on. Change anything you like: your edits are kept in this browser for every flyer you download, and Reset to my record puts them back.

4. Download and share

  1. Click Download story PNG (or Download post PNG), then post it, send it and share it.
Good to know
  • Events with a lock are staff only. Share those privately with brokers and clients, not on public channels.
  • Something off with the date, venue or photo? Those come from the event in Events, so ask the marketing team to update it, and your flyer follows.
  • Job title or BRN wrong? Ask HR to update your portal record.
  • Want the event's own contact on it instead of yours? Just turn off Add my contact card.
